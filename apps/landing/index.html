Credit infrastructure for RWA lenders

The model proposes.
The policy decides.

Saksi is a policy, underwriting, and attestation layer for invoice financiers and private-credit desks on Stellar. The AI scores and prices a receivable, deterministic limits bound what it can approve, and every decision — approval or rejection — gets a verifiable hash ready to anchor on Soroban.

View the repository Request demo access

Policy-bounded

Single-invoice cap, per-debtor exposure, and discount band are enforced outside the model. A bad prompt cannot exceed them.

Auditable by default

Every decision produces a canonical memo and SHA-256 hash. Rejections are attestable, not hidden.

Built for lenders

Software, not a balance sheet. The lender keeps custody, licensing, KYC, and collections.

How it works

01

Register a receivable

The lender submits an invoice reference, debtor id, amount, and discount. Caps are checked before anything is stored.

02

Score and price

A risk service returns a score, a price, and a rationale. Output is schema-validated; the model is advisory.

03

Anchor the decision

The API builds a canonical memo, hashes it, and stores a unique hash-to-invoice mapping. The Soroban contract mirrors the policy.

04

Settle and audit

Approved exposure stays reserved; rejected exposure is released. Anyone can recompute the memo and verify the hash.
